# Recipe and process sources

- PunkAPI v3: https://punkapi.dev/v3/beers (BrewDog DIY Dog recipes transcribed by a community project: https://github.com/alxiw/punkapi).
- Original DIY Dog v8: https://brewdogmedia.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/docs/2019+DIY+DOG+-+V8.pdf . PDF page 14, printed XIII, describes an all-grain brew day, recommending 60 minutes for most ales and 90 for most lagers. This is general advice, not a guaranteed boil time for each recipe. Pages 12–15 describe cleaning, equipment, mash, separation, boiling, cooling, aeration and pitching.
- AHA all-grain tutorial: https://homebrewersassociation.org/tutorials/all-grain-batch-sparge-homebrewing/all-grain-batch-sparge-homebrewing/ . Supports checking fermentation with gravity readings over several days rather than relying on a fixed number of days.
- AHA bottle conditioning: https://homebrewersassociation.org/how-to-brew/mastering-the-art-of-bottle-conditioning/ . Priming depends on the process and amount of beer; this project does not invent a universal priming dose.

The generated guides distinguish source facts from general process guidance. They are programmatic adaptations, not brewery-approved or expert-reviewed instructions. Exact source hop labels remain unchanged. Missing numeric boil time, conditioning and packaging specifics are listed explicitly; no recipe is labelled complete solely because it has mash and fermentation fields.

Original records, fetched PDF, datasets and model artifacts stay local. MIT licensing of this repository covers our code, not third-party material. Do not publish the corpus or model as licensed source material without checking the underlying rights.
